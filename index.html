Welcome to Baseball Limits 2D

The chart — each dot is one MLB season (or career total in Career mode), plotted on any two stats you choose. Dots are colored by era, from the Dead Ball years to today.

The red curve is the Pareto frontier: no player in history has ever achieved more in both stats at once. It's the outer edge of what's been possible.

Click any red dot to trace that player's other seasons in gold — a career trail pinned to the frontier.

Hover a red dot to see its Loneliness Radius — a dashed circle whose size shows how close any other season ever came. A wide circle means nothing in history even approached it. Click to lock it.

Press ▶ next to the year range to watch the frontier evolve — the end year advances automatically so you can see which seasons rewrote history and when.

Explore by picking any two stats, adjusting the year range, or filtering by league, handedness, or birthplace.
